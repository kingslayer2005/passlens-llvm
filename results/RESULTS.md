## 9. What these results do not show

- **Running time.** The objective is instruction count. A smaller function is not necessarily a faster one.
- **Causality.** SHAP describes what the model relies on, not what makes a pass work. Agreement with `heuristics.yaml` shows consistency, not that the model rediscovered LLVM's logic.
- **Generality.** Two C benchmark suites, 53 programs, with 8 programs holding most samples. Only 496 of 1118 source files compile outside their build systems.
- **Full phase ordering.** Each label is the effect of ONE pass on one state.
- **Independence of folds.** Cross-validation folds share training data, so the fold-level test is optimistic; the program bootstrap is the safer number.
- **Verified heuristics.** Entries of `heuristics.yaml` marked `verified: false` have not been checked against LLVM's source by hand.
